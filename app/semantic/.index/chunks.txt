## # Migration Auditor Knowledge Base

---

## SKU_NOT_FOUND
When an order line references a SKU that isn't present in your products master.
**Why it matters:** inventory allocation and fulfillment can’t proceed reliably.
**Fix:** ensure every ordered SKU exists in Products. If you changed SKUs between systems, create a mapping table.

---

## SKU_HAS_WHITESPACE
Whitespace in SKUs often breaks integrations and barcode workflows.
**Fix:** standardize to '-' or '_' and keep SKU stable.
